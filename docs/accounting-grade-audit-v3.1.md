# Accounting-Grade Audit v3.1 Remediation

## Scope and stop condition

This is a read-only remediation pass. No production schema, data, FNS auth/session, timers or historical analytics were changed. Production purchases, ledger postings and accounting backfill remain forbidden until another architecture review.

## Executive verdict

Evidence collection is `PROVISIONAL`. Certified accounting analytics are `BLOCKED`. The current POC is deterministic for receipt ingestion and raw/normalized reconciliation, but it does not yet model purchase, payment, fulfillment, return, correction, advance, credit or stored value as separate business events.

## Household direction

FFD operation semantics are from the cash-register user/seller perspective:

| `operationType` | FNS/KKT meaning | Household cash direction | Business event |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | receipt/приход | generally money out | sale/purchase evidence |
| 2 | return of receipt | generally money in | return/refund evidence |
| 3 | seller expense | generally money in | seller-to-buyer payment or other expense |
| 4 | return of seller expense | generally money out | reversal of seller expense |

These are three separate dimensions: fiscal operation direction, household cash direction and business event type. `operation_type=3` must not be labelled household expense.
